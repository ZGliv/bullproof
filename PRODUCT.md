## Platform
web

## Users
Zack uses this on a laptop and phone to discover interesting stocks and understand why they merit further research. These facts were explicitly established in the current conversation.

## Product Purpose
Find interesting companies with fresh research, real data, charts, source evidence, and clear reasons to investigate. Mixed investment horizons are wanted and must be labeled when grounded. The user rejected fictional examples, a fixed three-company showcase, prose-heavy presentation, and the current generic terminal design.

## Operating Context
Portfolio-linked web application with Safari home-screen support. Discovery leads into company research, source filings, watchlists, and notes.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Next.js web app, SEC-backed discovery and financial facts, ticker search, TradingView market charts, local watchlists and notes. Financial snapshots are dated; incomplete quarters are not invented. On October 28 the user selected Signal Atlas (B) for production discovery and Research Journal (A) for company briefs. The live implementation uses the SEC pipeline, not the fixed design-preview dataset. Global coverage and company catalysts are not yet complete. No unsupported buy recommendations, valuations, forecasts, or price histories.

## Brand Commitments
Bullproof is the approved name. On October 29, Zack selected the upward-facing three-quarter bull head with a check-shaped cut for the app logo and icon, replacing the B mark. The user wants research, substantial data visualization, and intuitive use.

## Product Principles
- Evidence and dates accompany financial claims.
- Visual discovery should make the reason a company is interesting clear.
- Real working behaviors matter more than decorative demos.
- Desktop and phone are both primary usage contexts.
